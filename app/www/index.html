    Quick guide

    • Inventory: all your items (name, category, quantity, locations) — your stock database. Starts empty — import starter-inventory-import.json via the ☰ menu for example items, or add your own.
    • Lists: individual packing lists for specific trips, filled with items from your inventory.
    • Bulk Add: add several items to one category at once.
    • Manage: rename/delete categories & locations, reset inventory.
    • ☰ menu: Export/Import as a backup file · "Link" saves changes automatically to a local file — stays linked across reloads (may need one click to confirm).
    • Header: 🔗 filename = actively linked, 🔗⏳ filename = link needs one click to confirm (a prompt appears automatically for this).
    • All data stays in this browser only until you export it.
    • Tap category/group headings to collapse/expand them.
